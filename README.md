## about me

my name is nyx. i'm a thirtysomething hobbyist developer. i use she/her, they/them, or it/its pronouns. i'm autistic and my special interest is computer science, and everything i know about computers is self-taught. currently learning c with the goal of eventually learning assembly after a decade of working primarily with javascript and php and a brief foray into game development. i learned to code to make tumblr themes in the early '10s as a teenager, discovered i enjoyed coding, and got into web dev as a hobby in my 20s. then i worked with the godot engine a bit and learned its programming language, discovered i prefer strictly typed languages when i never used a single loosely typed variable in gdscript, and then realized i was interested in learning more low-level concepts.

i'm now working on my first actual piece of software, autvox. it's a command-line based alternative and augmented communication software, which, as an autistic person who sometimes experiences muteness, was something i felt i needed. i don't know how many other projects i'll ever have on here, if i'm being honest. i mainly code to learn about programming concepts, not so much to build things for people to use. i'm planning to make a TUI journal/diary to learn ncurses and file handling, which are skills i need to implement planned features in autvox, so maybe that will be on here someday.

as for my non-computer interests, i like long walks in the desert and candle-lit metal workshops. if you understood that reference, we're best friends now. non-negotiable. (that was a joke, just to be clear. i have no interest in making friends on github.) so clearly, i like fallout: new vegas (if you didn't get the reference, it's a line from veronica in the game). i'm definitely a gamer, or at least i consider myself so, though i despise massively multiplayer games. i play video games to get away from other real people, thank you very much. i read a lot: fiction, non-fiction, and fan-fiction. my favorite novel is pet sematary, though carrie is a close second. i'll give you three guesses as to my favorite author. i also write, and am currently working on a novel. i love music; i primarily listen to metalcore, industrial, or nu-metal, though i also like a lot of post-hardcore, emo, punk, pop-punk, and edm.

## a note about disability and code maintenance

i'm neurodevelopmentally and physically disabled which makes me unable to work. there are probably going to be months-long gaps where i don't have the ability to maintain my projects. that is the reality of being disabled. i am autistic with level 2 support needs as well as having ADHD, and i have at least two diagnosed conditions which affect my mobility (some symptoms are unclear as to their origin) as well as various chronic illnesses and chronic pain and fatigue, so i am very inconsistent in my ability to work on coding. i am also currently in autistic burnout and do not know when it will end, so please do not expect timely responses to any issues. if you find a bug and it is causing you serious issues, make a pull request if you think you can fix it. i'm more likely to accept your help in fixing the bug than to have the capacity fix it myself if i'm unable to respond to an issue.
